z3d plays The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D on a Linux desktop as PC
games: one window, one screen, a gamepad or keyboard and mouse, with the community's mods and our
own additions set up for you from your own files. It is a small app around the Azahar emulator's
core.

## What you need to bring

Nothing from the games and none of the third-party mods is in this download. z3d asks for two
folders the first time it starts and builds each game's setup from what it finds there.

Your own decrypted dumps (`.3ds` or `.cci`) of:

- Ocarina of Time 3D (USA)
- Majora's Mask 3D (USA) (Rev 1)

The mods, downloaded by you into one folder (a `.zip` or `.tar` can stay as it is; unpack a `.7z`
or `.rar` first):

- For Ocarina of Time 3D:
  - The One Screen alpha 1.1.12 by toadalle: https://github.com/toadalle/The-One-Screen
    (commit 891b60e; its `load/mods/0004000000033500` folder)
  - Henriko Magnifico's OoT3D 4K pack 5.0a, the 1080p download:
    https://henrikomagnifico.com/zelda-ocarina-of-time-3d-4k
- For Majora's Mask 3D:
  - Project Restoration v1.5.9 by leoetlino (its `v110/citra` folder):
    https://github.com/leoetlino/project-restoration/releases
  - Henriko Magnifico's MM3D 4K pack 3.0b-1, the 1080p download:
    https://henrikomagnifico.com/zelda-majoras-mask-3d-4k

z3d checks each file against the exact version it was made for. If one is missing or is another
version, the setup screen names it and where to get it.

## What it does

- A title picker that reads the games from your dump folder and sets each one up on its first
  start (about half a minute per game), with per-game options.
- Single screen. Ocarina of Time 3D uses The One Screen; Majora's Mask 3D keeps its menus on the
  touch screen, so z3d lays them over the top screen when they open.
- A play HUD of its own: hearts, magic, rupees, keys and the four face buttons with the items on
  them, shown with Xbox pad glyphs or key caps, whichever you used last.
- Gamepad, keyboard and mouse: the right stick is the camera, the mouse aims in first person
  (bow, Slingshot, looking around), LB or Tab reaches a second page of item buttons, and the
  number keys press item slots directly.
- Start skips cutscenes in both games.
- Ocarina of Time 3D: Quick Text, a free camera on the right stick, optional autosave after each
  scene change.
- Stepped stairs in both games in place of the flat ramps (can be turned off).
- The 4K texture packs, with the button prompts in their menus redrawn for the pad.
- Mods can be swapped while a game runs: F5 reloads our patch, F6 takes it out or puts it back,
  F7 turns the texture pack off or on. F10 or the guide button returns to the picker.

## Running it

Download `z3d-x86_64.AppImage`, make it executable and start it:

```
chmod +x z3d-x86_64.AppImage
./z3d-x86_64.AppImage
```

It needs a 64-bit x86 Linux desktop with glibc 2.39 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 40
and later), a Vulkan driver, and FUSE for the AppImage (`fusermount3`); without FUSE, start it
with `--appimage-extract-and-run`. Saves, settings and the setups it builds are kept in
`z3d` under your data folder (`$XDG_DATA_HOME`, normally `.local/share` in your home folder).

## Known gaps

- The item-slot layout is in progress: which buttons and keys carry which slots, and how the HUD
  shows them, will still change, and it differs between the two games.
- Linux x86-64 only.
- Only the USA dumps named above are supported.
- A texture pack changed on disk is picked up on the next start of the game, not by F7.
