z3d plays The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D on a Linux desktop as PC
games: one window, one screen, a gamepad or keyboard and mouse, with the community's mods and our
own additions set up for you from your own files. It is a small app around the Azahar emulator's
core.

What you need to bring and how to run it are the same as for v0.1.0: your own USA dumps of both
games, the four mods named in the setup screen, and a 64-bit x86 Linux desktop with glibc 2.39 or
newer, Vulkan and FUSE. Download `z3d-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and start it. Saves and
settings from v0.1.0 carry over.

## New since v0.1.0

- Majora's Mask 3D: Quick Text. The whole text box shows at once (Options).
- Majora's Mask 3D: optional autosave after each scene change (Options, off by default). It
  makes the same save an owl statue makes, at the entrance of the scene you just entered, so
  loading the file puts you there at the time you had. It does not save in the final hours of
  the third day, and it never replaces the Song of Time's save.
- The mouse turns the camera in both games, not only the aim in first person: Ocarina of Time 3D's
  free camera and Majora's Mask 3D's own camera follow the mouse the way they follow the right
  stick. In menus and on the item screens the mouse is the stylus again.
- The games' own text is drawn from outline fonts through the texture pack, so it stays sharp at
  any window size.
- The games' prompts are reworded for our inputs: the text names the pad's buttons or, after a
  key, the keyboard's caps (the core swaps in a key-cap variant of the prompt sheet), and the
  touch-screen prompts name our inputs in both games.
- The menus' hints (picker, options, folder browser) follow the device you used last, and Menu
  opens the item screen.
- Controls: the keyboard's R opens the ocarina's song list, the right and middle mouse buttons
  change pages on the item screens, and in Majora's Mask 3D View on the map opens the Bombers'
  Notebook. If you change a key binding, the picker offers to set the game up again so the
  prompts match.
- Item slots: the HUD's prompts name each slot's button; Majora's Mask 3D's map, ocarina and
  Gear verbs work from the pad and keyboard; the Song of Time plays only while its slot is held;
  an Ocarina of Time 3D slot is pressed once Link's hands are free.
- Fixes: the app closes on SIGTERM even when its compositor has gone.

## Known gaps

- The item-slot layout is still in progress and differs between the two games.
- No mouse sensitivity option yet, and the mouse buttons do not fire aimed items.
- Linux x86-64 only, and only the USA dumps named in the setup screen.
- A texture pack changed on disk is picked up on the next start of the game, not by F7.
