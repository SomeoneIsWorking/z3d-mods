z3d plays The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D on a Linux desktop as PC
games: one window, one screen, a gamepad or keyboard and mouse, with the community's mods and our
own additions set up for you from your own files. It is a small app around the Azahar emulator's
core.

What you need to bring and how to run it are the same as before: your own USA dumps of both
games, the four mods named in the setup screen, and a 64-bit x86 Linux desktop with glibc 2.39 or
newer, Vulkan and FUSE. Download `z3d-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and start it. Saves and
settings from v0.2.0 carry over. Each game asks to be set up again once, since its setup changed.

## New since v0.2.0

- Mouse sensitivity: each game's options have a "Mouse sensitivity" row, from 0.25x to 4x, for
  the camera and for aiming.
- The left mouse button fires the item you are aiming (the Slingshot, the bow) and stays the
  sword otherwise. RT on the pad does the same while you aim.
- The word under the A button on our HUD is in the console's language: French and Spanish get
  their own words instead of the English ones.
- French and Spanish: the games' button prompts are reworded for our controls in both languages,
  as the English ones were, and Majora's Mask 3D's French prompts show pad buttons and key caps
  too.
- Majora's Mask 3D: the Gear prompt names Pause (LB and Menu, or Esc).
- A HUD ring whose item changes when you hold LB turns over like a card, so you can see the
  change; its button prompt stays readable throughout.

## Known gaps

- Mouse aim in Ocarina of Time 3D's Master Quest was not checked; the mouse camera there was.
- Linux x86-64 only, and only the USA dumps named in the setup screen.
- A texture pack changed on disk is picked up on the next start of the game, not by F7.
