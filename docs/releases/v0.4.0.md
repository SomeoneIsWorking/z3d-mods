z3d plays The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D on a Linux desktop as PC
games: one window, one screen, a gamepad or keyboard and mouse, with the community's mods and our
own additions set up for you from your own files. It is a small app around the Azahar emulator's
core.

What you need to bring and how to run it are the same as before: your own USA dumps of both
games, the four mods named in the setup screen, and a 64-bit x86 Linux desktop with glibc 2.39 or
newer, Vulkan and FUSE. Download `z3d-x86_64.AppImage`, `chmod +x` it and start it. Saves and
settings from v0.3.0 carry over. A game whose setup changed asks to be set up again once.

## New since v0.3.0

- The keyboard has its own HUD: a bar of item slots 1 to 8 under their number keys, the sword on
  the left mouse button and the action on E. It shows while you play on the keyboard and the
  pad's HUD shows while you play on the pad.
- The keyboard has no chord any more: Shift+1 to 3 are gone. Keys 1 to 8 are slots 1 to 8, and
  the four slots behind LB are the pad's.
- The D-pad's four slots sit under the hearts instead of beside the face buttons.
- HUD size (0.5x to 1.5x) and HUD opacity (25% to 100%) in each game's options. Each corner's
  part of the HUD grows or shrinks toward its own corner.
- An item's count turns green when it is full, at the game's own capacity for it (your bag or
  quiver), as on the N64.
- Majora's Mask 3D:
  - Its minimap is on the top screen's bottom right, where Ocarina of Time 3D's is, sized to
    fit one box in every scene.
  - Small keys show above the rupees in a temple, as in Ocarina of Time 3D. Neither game
    shows a key count before the first key.
  - The ice and light arrows show the quiver's count, and the powder keg its own.

## Known gaps

- The game's own clock in Majora's Mask 3D is still the game's art, not our vector HUD.
- Mouse aim in Ocarina of Time 3D's Master Quest was not checked; the mouse camera there was.
- Linux x86-64 only, and only the USA dumps named in the setup screen.
- A texture pack changed on disk is picked up on the next start of the game, not by F7.
