Zelda 3D: Revamped (z3d) plays The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D on a
desktop as PC games: one window, one screen, a gamepad or keyboard and mouse, now at 60 fps, with
the community's mods and our own additions set up for you from your own files. It is a small app
around the Azahar emulator's core.

Bring your own USA dumps of both games and the four mods named in the setup screen.

- Linux: `z3d-x86_64.AppImage`, for a 64-bit x86 desktop with glibc 2.39 or newer, Vulkan and
  FUSE. `chmod +x` it and start it.
- macOS (new): `z3d-macos-arm64.zip`, for Apple silicon. Unzip it and open `z3d.app`. It is
  signed ad hoc, not by Apple: the first time, Control-click it and choose Open.

Saves and settings from v0.4.0 carry over. Each game asks to be set up again once.

## New since v0.4.0

- 60 fps in both games. Each in-between frame is the game's own drawing run again with the
  camera, characters, their limbs, fades, animated textures and effects halfway between two
  ticks. The game's logic keeps its own 30 a second. Scene changes, doors and cutscene shots are
  never blended across. "Frame interpolation" in each game's options, on by default.
- Stepped stairs: the games' flat painted ramps drawn and walked as stone steps, or as painted
  3D steps where the texture is wood, sand or earth. "Stepped stairs" in the options: Off,
  Small, Medium or Large.
- The title picker shows both games running side by side on a slanted edge. The one you pick
  plays on without a reload.
- Fullscreen is a saved choice, on by default, with an options row, F11 and Alt+Enter.
- The HUD follows more of the game: Epona's carrots, the View icon, a minigame's lent item, slots
  dimmed while the game takes no item.
- Ocarina of Time 3D's file select shows its date in HD.
- A 16:10 screen shows the whole HUD, with bars above and below.

## Known gaps

- No Windows or Android download yet. Windows builds are in progress.
- The game's own clock in Majora's Mask 3D is still the game's art, not our vector HUD.
- Only the USA dumps named in the setup screen.
